THUNDER OFFICE - .GLB ASSET PROMPTS (team.wondermayank.in)
=========================================================
Use with any text-to-3D / image-to-3D tool (Meshy, Tripo, Rodin/Hyper3D, Luma Genie, Spline AI) or a Blender workflow.
The website already works without any .glb (it draws simple placeholder furniture). Every file you add REPLACES the placeholder props of that room.

1) STYLE PROMPT (paste at the start of EVERY prompt)
----------------------------------------------------
"Soft clay 3D render style, chunky rounded shapes, smooth matte plasticine material, no sharp edges, low-poly friendly (under 15k triangles),
pastel palette: soft purple #7C3AED #A78BFA #C4B5FD, blue #60A5FA #B9D9DC, white and off-white base, subtle warm wood accents.
Clean minimal design, game-ready, single object, no text, no logos, no people, no background, centred on the floor."

2) ASSET CONTRACT (very important)
----------------------------------
- Format: .glb (glTF 2.0 binary), embedded textures, Y-up, 1 unit = 1 metre.
- Size: each file under 5 MB (Cloudflare Pages limit is 25 MB per file; keep phones in mind: total models under ~25 MB).
- Compress: gltf-transform optimize in.glb out.glb --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp   (the site loads Meshopt-compressed files).
- One file PER ROOM, named exactly:  models/decor_<roomId>.glb
- Origin of the file = CENTRE of the room, ON the floor (y = 0).
- Room axes: -Z is the BACK wall (where the big picture hangs), +Z is the DOOR side, -X is the LEFT wall (member portraits / photos hang there), +X is the right wall.
- Keep the walls, floor, ceiling, doors and pictures OUT of the file; only furniture and small decor. Do not put big objects within 1.2 m of the door (+Z) and keep the centre aisle free so the visitor can walk in.
- Furniture height must stay below 1.1 m on the back-wall side (the picture hangs at y 1.1 to 2.8 m and the plaque at y 0.4-0.8 m, centred).
- There is no collision with props in the prototype, so keep props pushed to the sides.
- Register the files in models/manifest.json:
    { "decor": ["living","tech","designer","creativity","singing","dancing","ca","wondermayank","anamika", "..."], "hub": true }
  and the hub file models/hub_decor.glb (origin = centre of the hub, floor level).
